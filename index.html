Our Story

Welcome to कथा FOTO — photography for birthdays, family moments, celebrations and the little memories that deserve to last. Based in Oslo and rooted in the Himalaya, we carry two homes into every frame: the mountains and heritage of Nepal, and the quiet light of Norway. Every session is a katha — a story — waiting to be told.
